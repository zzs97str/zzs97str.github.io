Visitor counts are provided by Busuanzi. They estimate unique visitors rather than page views. Counts are approximate: a shared network, a new device, or a changed IP address can affect how visitors are distinguished.

Exclude your own visits

On each browser you use to visit your website, choose Exclude this browser once. Future visits from that browser will not be submitted to the counter. This settings page never submits visits.

Checking this browser's preference…

The preference is saved only in this browser's website data. Set it again after clearing that data, switching browser profiles, or using another device. Private browsing does not retain the preference after its session ends.

View the count

Open site statistics to view the service's statistics page while keeping this browser excluded. New visits are counted after this feature is enabled; previous visits cannot be reconstructed.
